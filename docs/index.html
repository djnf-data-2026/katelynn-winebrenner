A close race

In the 2024 Frederick County school board election, the Frederick County Democratic Party endorsed four candidates for three open at-large seats. This decision caused splintering within the party that led to conservative candidates – Jamie Kiersten Brennan and Colt Morningstar Black – to take two of the seats.

Brennan stepped down as chair of the county chapter of Moms for Liberty to run for school board seat. She won 16.8% of the total votes– the highest in the county.

Janie Monier was the only candidate with support from the Democratic party to win, racking up 16.7% – the second highest percentage of votes.

Black ran with conservative support. He won 16.2% of the votes – the third top candidate.

Josh Bokee lost the race with 16.1% of the total votes. Chad King Wilson Sr. also lost with 14.8%. Veronica Lowe earned the least at 12.5%.

Split precincts

As Frederick County grows in population, the political ideology has started to shift to the left. Vice President Kamala Harris won the county in the 2024 presidential election with almost 53% of votes, compared to President Donald Trump’s 44% of votes.

Their performance was closely tied to the geography of the county. Harris performed well in and around the City of Frederick, and Trump performed well in the rural sides of the county.

In rural precincts that voted overwhelmingly for Trump, Black and Brennan performed well.

Democrat-backed candidates performed well in the city, but in precincts where Harris narrowly outperformed Trump, the vote for those candidates split, allowing Brennan and Black to place in the top three.

Harris won 39 of the county’s 66 precincts.

Among the precincts to support Harris, 15 of them also voted to elect at least one of the conservative-supported school board candidates.

Relevance

This demonstrates that, despite Frederick’s growth in recent years that have skewed its politics to the left, the conservative values in the county still remain strong when it comes to local government.

Identifying where the split among voters happened will better contextualize the upcoming school board election for readers, parents and candidates. It will be important to keep an eye on what the Democrats do differently this election season as a result.
